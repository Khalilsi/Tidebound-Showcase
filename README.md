## 🌊 THE GAME

**Welcome to Tidebound.**

Deep beneath the surface, every passage holds a challenge, every obstacle demands a solution, and every step brings you closer to escape.

Tidebound is a **3D underwater escape and puzzle adventure** where players explore submerged environments, solve puzzles, interact with their surroundings, and overcome environmental hazards to progress through challenging areas.

Developed collaboratively by a team of three, the project combines gameplay programming, 3D modeling, and asset integration to create an immersive underwater game experience.
