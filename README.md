## THE EXPERIENCE

Tidebound is a 3D underwater escape and puzzle game. Explore submerged environments, solve puzzles, navigate hazards, and make progress through challenging areas. The project brings together gameplay programming, 3D modeling, and game-asset integration in an atmospheric underwater setting.

## GAMEPLAY

| 🌊 **Explore** | 🧩 **Solve** | ⚠️ **Survive** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Navigate underwater environments and discover different game areas. | Solve puzzles and overcome challenges to move forward. | Encounter environmental hazards throughout the game. |

| 🔦 **Illuminate** | 🚩 **Progress** | ⚙️ **Interact** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Use a flashlight as part of the gameplay experience. | Checkpoints support player progression. | Interactive game systems and custom-designed UI support the gameplay experience. |
